# Castaway Keys Live

A falling-notes piano trainer for a 2-octave toy piano (C to C, color-coded letters).
It listens through your phone's microphone, scores each note, and sets the shore on fire when you get a streak going.

Play it: https://mbwood3.github.io/castaway-keys/

- Tap **🎤 Set up listening**, allow the mic, then play C, E and G so it tunes itself to your piano.
- **Wait for me** pauses each note until it hears the right one.
- Hit 10 in a row to light every torch and catch fire (×2, ×3 at 25, ×4 at 40).
- After each song you get a report on timing, wrong notes and trouble spots, plus a practice loop that speeds up as you get it clean.

All songs are public domain or traditional.
